Commodity Specification & Grading
AgmarkNet Grade Benchmark

Grade Standards: Tomatoes & Solanaceous Crops

Official grading parameters specified by the Directorate of Marketing & Inspection (DMI), Government of India for wholesale mandi dispatch.

Grade A (Premium)

Firm, uniform deep red color, free from skin blemishes, cracks or rot. Moisture content 90-93%. Minimum diameter 50mm.

Grade B (Standard)

Slightly irregular shape permitted, up to 5% minor surface scarring allowed. Suitable for standard retail and regional transit.

Grade C (Processing)

Soft or overripe specimens suitable for pulp, puree, or ketchup processing. No rotting or foul odor.

Mandi Deductions & Tare Weight Rules

  • Standard Crate Tare: Plastic crates tare deducted at exactly 1.8 kg per crate.
  • Moisture Allowance: Tolerable moisture variance is 2% above base index without rate penalty.
  • Commission Agent Fee (Adath): Under Maharashtra APMC Act, buyer pays the mandi cess; farmers are legally exempt from commission fee deductions.
