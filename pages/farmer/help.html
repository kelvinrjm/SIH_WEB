Frequently Asked Questions

How do I list my crops for sale?
Click Add Product in the sidebar menu. Fill in the crop details, upload a photo or use your device camera, set your expected price, and click Publish Produce. Your listing becomes instantly visible to verified buyers across the state.
How does the Market Price discovery work?
FarmLink queries live APMC / Mandi market prices published by the Ministry of Agriculture (AgmarkNet). If live mandi data is temporarily unavailable or your district API key is not configured, the portal displays an explicit unverified state with a retry option — ensuring you are never misled by fake rates.
What should I do when I receive an order or buyer request?
Open the Orders page or Buyer Requests page. You can review the buyer's requested quantity, proposed price, delivery destination, and contact number. Click Confirm to accept, or Decline if you cannot fulfill the order.
How does payment and delivery work?
Direct farmer-to-buyer transactions support both online escrow and Cash on Delivery (COD). Once you mark the order as Out for Delivery, the buyer tracks logistics in real-time, and settlement occurs upon delivery verification.
