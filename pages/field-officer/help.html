Field Officer SOP & Manual

Field Assistance Manual & SOP

Standard operational procedures for onboarding farmers, verifying crops, capturing photographs, and assisting with market fulfillment.

1. Farmer Registration & Identity Verification

Field Officers must verify the farmer's active phone number and location before registering. Search existing records to avoid duplicate registrations. The farmer must understand that they are the legal owner and seller of the produce listed.

2. Crop & Produce Name Direct Input

Always type the exact produce name (e.g. Tomato, Onion, Coconut, Rice, Groundnut) into the standard text box. Do not constrain the farmer to predefined crop categories. The multilingual dictionary automatically resolves localized names in English, Tamil, and Hindi.

3. Photography & AI Vision Verification Rules

Take at least 1–3 clear, well-lit photos of the actual harvested crop. Avoid extreme blur, shadows, or stock photography. Cloudflare Workers AI will inspect the produce for freshness and quality grading.

4. AgmarkNet Mandi Rate Guidance

Use the "Check Live Mandi Rate" tool to show the farmer prevailing minimum, modal, and maximum prices in the nearest APMC yard. Never overwrite the farmer's desired selling price without their explicit agreement.

5. Verification Decisions & Marketplace Badging

Once verified, the listing is tagged with FIELD VERIFIED and displayed in the shared Buyer Marketplace. If rejecting a listing, select or record a clear constructive reason so the farmer can remedy the issue.
