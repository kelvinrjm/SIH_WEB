Frequently Asked Questions

How does FarmLink verify the authenticity of farmer listings?
Farmers are verified against state land records (7/12 extracts) and PM-KISAN database IDs. Every lot published includes exact GPS coordinate stamps and village APMC mandi benchmarks.
What happens if the delivered consignment has quality defects?
In Cash on Delivery (COD) and Escrow models, funds are not released until you inspect the lot at your receiving dock. If there is a grade variance, you can report a discrepancy within 2 hours of unloading.
Can I request custom bulk tonnage or negotiable prices?
Yes. On every produce details page, click Submit Custom Offer. This allows you to propose custom volume (e.g. 5 tonnes) and target rates directly to the grower.
Are invoices eligible for GST and APMC mandi compliance?
Yes. Every order generates a downloadable legal tax invoice and settlement bill specifying the agricultural produce exemption, mandi cess, and farmer 7/12 cluster origin.
