How FarmLink Operates

A simple, transparent 4-step workflow connecting cultivators directly with retail merchants and commercial bulk buyers.

Farmer Experience

For Farmers & Sellers

1

List Produce using Voice or Form

Speak naturally in Marathi, Tamil, Hindi, or English (e.g. "500 kg Tomato in Nashik at 25 rupees") or fill the guided form.

2

Upload Real Lot Photos

Take photos of your produce from camera or gallery with instant magic-byte validation to prove quality grade and firmness.

3

Compare Live Mandi Benchmark

Check real AgmarkNet modal prices from your nearest APMC market yard so you price your crop competitively with zero middleman deductions.

4

Confirm Order & Get Direct Settlement

Receive instant notifications when buyers place orders. Dispatch the lot from your farm gate and receive direct payment upon delivery.

Buyer Experience

For Commercial & Retail Buyers

1

Browse Verified Farm Lots

Filter by district, agricultural sector, variety, and quality grade (Grade A / B / C) across Maharashtra.

2

Stock-Validated Cart & 3-Step Checkout

Real-time stock reservation prevents overselling. Select delivery destination and choose between Cash on Delivery (COD) or Escrow.

3

Live 5-Stage Logistics Tracking

Follow consignment status in real-time from farm packaging to transit vehicle arrival at your godown.

4

Download Legal Tax Invoices

Instant computer-generated APMC mandi settlement bills and GST tax invoices for commercial accounting.
