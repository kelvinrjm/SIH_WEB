Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding mandi rates, produce listings, logistics, and legal compliance.

What is FarmLink and how is it related to Govt. of Maharashtra?
FarmLink is developed under Smart India Hackathon 2026 problem statement SIH26132 sponsored by the Government of Maharashtra. It establishes direct agricultural market linkage between verified farmers and commercial buyers to eliminate middlemen markups and provide transparent price discovery based on real AgmarkNet data.
Are the mandi market prices real or estimated?
Prices are queried directly from the Government of India's Open Government Data platform (data.gov.in / AgmarkNet). When live government mandi API connectivity is not configured or data is unavailable, FarmLink honestly displays an explicit unverified state — it never fabricates or hallucinates fake rates.
How do farmers receive payment for their produce?
Transactions support Cash on Delivery (settled upon physical weighment at the buyer’s dock) or instant Escrow UPI/bank transfer where funds are secured at order placement and released immediately upon delivery sign-off.
Can farmers use voice input in their mother tongue?
Yes. The Kisan Voice AI feature supports Tamil (தமிழ்), Hindi (हिन्दी), and English speech recognition, allowing farmers to list produce or search mandi rates hands-free with large, accessible buttons.
How are transport and vehicle pickups arranged?
Farmers mark their farm gate coordinate on an interactive map. Buyers choose between arranging their own carrier or selecting direct farm dispatch, where a verified commercial carrier (e.g. Mahindra Bolero Pickup or truck) collects the lot directly from the farm.
